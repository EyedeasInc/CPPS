A celebration of the Power Platform

The Canadian Power Platform Summit helps makers, developers and IT teams get more out of Microsoft's Power Platform. We're a non-profit, run by the community, and every session is built so people can learn from each other and take something real back to work.

Our first summit was a hit. A fantastic turnout of attendees from across Canada and beyond dove into insightful sessions and epic networking, guided by expert speakers who made every session educational and inspiring.

Building on that success, we're even more pumped for the next one. On March 13, 2027, the day before the Microsoft MVP Summit, we're welcoming top-notch international speakers for another unforgettable experience in beautiful Vancouver.

A day of learning, building & connecting

Talks from people who ship Power Platform work, a sponsor floor, and plenty of time to meet each other. Every skill level is welcome.

CPPS is ideal for anyone passionate about leveraging the Microsoft Power Platform to drive business change: business analysts, developers, IT professionals, data scientists, and decision-makers across every industry.
