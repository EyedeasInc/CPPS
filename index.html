🍁 About CPPS

Empowering Canada's Power Platform community

A celebration of the Power Platform

At the Canadian Power Platform Summit, our mission is to empower individuals and organizations through the innovative use of Microsoft's Power Platform. As a non-profit, we create an inclusive, collaborative environment where attendees learn, share, and grow their skills.

Our inaugural summit was an absolute hit — a fantastic turnout of attendees from across Canada and beyond dove into insightful sessions, hands-on workshops, and epic networking, guided by expert speakers who made every session educational and inspiring.

Building on that success, we're even more pumped for the next one. Set for March 12–13, 2027 — right before the Microsoft MVP Summit — we're welcoming top-notch international speakers for another unforgettable experience in beautiful Vancouver.

Frequently Asked Questions

CPPS is ideal for anyone passionate about leveraging the Microsoft Power Platform to drive business transformation and innovation — business analysts, developers, IT professionals, data scientists, and decision-makers across every industry.

A dynamic mix of insightful sessions, hands-on workshops, and networking opportunities. Learn from expert speakers, gain practical experience with Power Platform tools, and connect with like-minded professionals to exchange ideas and best practices.

CPPS 2027 will be held in Vancouver, British Columbia — that much is confirmed! We're finalizing the exact downtown venue and will announce it soon. Vancouver is known for its stunning natural scenery, diverse culture, and thriving tech scene.

The $299 fee helps cover the costs of organizing and delivering a high-quality workshop — venue, materials, and logistics.
